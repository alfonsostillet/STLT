# ISF shaders that come with STLT

These effects and sources are ISF shaders (Interactive Shader Format, https://isf.video) from VIDVOX's collection,
https://github.com/Vidvox/ISF-Files (commit 395072d48b3ce7351ccb20a5fda54470591324df), under the MIT license in
`LICENSE` next to this file. They are unchanged.

Only shaders written by VIDVOX's own people (credited to VIDVOX, zoidberg or Carter Rosenberg) are here, and none
that say they come from code under another or an unclear licence (Shadertoy, glslsandbox, v002, blog posts, other
add-ons). Left out as well: those that need image files of their own or the sound as a texture, which STLT does
not feed yet.

More ISF shaders work too: put the `.fs` files (with their `.vs`, if any) in the `isf` folder of STLT's settings
folder, or in the system's ISF folder (`C:\ProgramData\ISF` on Windows, `/Library/Graphics/ISF` on macOS).
