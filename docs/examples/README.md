# Example setups

- **Test stage.json**: an Advanced Output setup for trying the editor. Screen 1 is an LED processor (2048 × 768) with a 6 × 3 cabinet wall and two wings, 24 slices; Right wing 2 sits 6 px too high, so the Map step reports an overlap. Screens 2 and 3 are two projectors with keystone and soft edges; screen 4 goes out by NDI. The Grid setup's Stage (step 3) has a façade 20 × 12 m, the ground and a stage block, with the two projectors 24 m away lighting the façade and overlapping in the middle. Every screen starts on Virtual: drag them onto your displays in step 1.

To load it, put the file in the `output-presets` folder of the settings folder (Preferences > General shows where it is; on Windows `%APPDATA%\STLT\output-presets`), then choose it in the Advanced Output under *Presets and imports > Load preset*.
